# parthkabra.me

The content of Parth's personal website: what gets written and how pieces of it relate.

## Microblog

**Micro post**:
A short, dated piece of writing in the Microblog, with an optional title.
_Avoid_: Note, tweet, update

**Thread**:
A micro post that starts it, plus the micro posts that continue it, read together oldest first.
_Avoid_: Conversation, series

**Reply**:
A micro post that starts its own Thread while answering or following up on a micro post in another Thread.
_Avoid_: Follow-up, quote, continuation

**Context line**:
The line above a micro post shown outside its Thread that says where it comes from: the Thread it belongs to, or the micro post it's a Reply to.
_Avoid_: Breadcrumb, label
